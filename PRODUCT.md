# GoStudios Real Hub

## Product truth

The GoStudios Real Hub is an independent, research-first directory for public projects published by the GoStudios-Real GitHub organization. It helps visitors understand what each project does and move directly to its live page, download, or source repository.

The catalogue spans games, creative tools, companion apps, and desktop experiments. Each listing must distinguish a live product from a source-only project or a concept, and must not imply affiliation with GoStudios, Microsoft, Roblox, or other third parties.

## Audience and job

Visitors arrive curious about a project they have seen elsewhere or browse the organization for something to play, try, or inspect. They need a quick, honest explanation and a clear next action without learning the organization’s repository structure first.

## Success

A first-time visitor can scan the featured projects, recognize the one that fits their interest, and open its live destination or source with minimal friction. The full public catalogue and research notes remain available for deeper exploration.

## Constraints

- Keep all project names, descriptions, capabilities, and destination URLs accurate to the public source material.
- Keep the page a dependency-free static site that works on GitHub Pages.
- Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion support.
- AI is mentioned only where the corresponding project actually includes AI functionality.
- Clearly label concepts and source-only projects; do not invent live launches or endorsements.
