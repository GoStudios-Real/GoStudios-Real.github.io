# Surface brief: index.html

- **Scope and mode:** The main one-page project launch hub; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** A first-time visitor scanning the studio's public projects for something to play, try, or inspect.
- **Action and proof:** Match a destination to its concise, factual description, then launch the live page or open the source.
- **Constraints:** Keep eight featured project destinations, public claims accurate, source-only status explicit, the site dependency-free, and navigation accessible at mobile widths.
- **Chosen direction:** An arcade-expo field guide composed as an eight-slot collector atlas. The memorable moment is the clickable route board, which shows every destination before the visitor scrolls.
- **Unresolved decisions:** None.
